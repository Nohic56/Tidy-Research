Tidy Research

Your links and research shortcuts, with clear controls.

Browsing preferences

Runs before ordinary GET page requests. Does not modify form submissions, images, scripts, or background requests. Existing tabs can be cleaned from the toolbar without reloading.

Queries stay in your browser until you choose a shortcut. Your default search engine stays as you set it.

One hostname per line, such as example.org. Also pauses its subdomains. Paused websites are left unchanged by both automatic and manual cleanup.

Try a link

Paste a link to see the result.

Recognised utm_* fields and common advertising click IDs are removed. Other fields, empty values, fragments, ordering and query encoding are preserved. Links with recognised signing, login, or token parameters are left unchanged. Unrecognised signing schemes may require a site exception.

Research shortcuts

In Firefox’s address bar, type u Space then your query. Enter opens UniProt. The dropdown also offers PubMed and Maps.

  • u P04637 opens a UniProt accession directly.
  • u CDC27_HUMAN searches an entry name.
  • u cyclin B Xenopus searches UniProt.
  • u pubmed APC/C phosphorylation searches PubMed.

Accession recognition checks the published format; it does not verify that an entry exists. A gene name alone can refer to several proteins or species.

Select text on a page and right-click for research searches. Right-click a link to copy its clean address.

Privacy & permissions

No analytics, advertising, accounts, remote code, or background research queries. URL cleanup and identifier recognition run locally.

When you explicitly open a research shortcut, the query is sent to the website you chose: UniProt, PubMed, or Google Maps. That destination receives a normal browser request and applies its own privacy policy. Firefox discloses this as access to search terms during installation.

Website access is needed to clean new navigations across websites and display search shortcuts. Page editing is used only when you choose “Clean address”. Clipboard permission is used only when you choose a copy action. No cookie, password, download, or browsing-history API is used.

Preferences and excluded hostnames are stored locally. The most recent cleaned address per tab is stored in browser-session memory for the undo action; it is not synced or sent to the developer. Undo is offered for 10 minutes on that same page. Records are replaced by later cleanup, removed when tabs close, and cleared when Firefox exits.

Firefox’s protected pages cannot be edited. Private windows require a separate allowance in Firefox. Cleanup cannot erase tracking information that was already sent before the extension acted.
